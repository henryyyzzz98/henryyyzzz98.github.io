Banked$0

    A secret number from 1 to 100 is drawn when the game starts and never changes. Each round you lock in a range. If the number is inside it, you bank the prize and your range becomes the next round's scale. If it isn't, the game is over. From Round 2 on you can walk away with what you've banked, then make one fun guess to see what would have happened. Drag or tap the scale, or use the arrow keys, to move your range.
