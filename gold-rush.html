How to play
  • Main game: players take turns picking from 25 numbered claims. 18 hide cash from $500 to $5,000, 2 are X2 (doubles your total) and 5 are Fool's Gold (your total drops to $0).
  • Open a lead of $25,000 or more and you win on the spot. Otherwise, once every cash claim is open, the higher total wins. A dead heat means a fresh board for both players.
  • Super Rush: the winner faces 15 claims: three each of $10,000, $25,000 and $100,000, plus six Fool's Gold. Match three of the same cash amount before three Fool's Gold to win it.
  • Match three in your first three picks and the prize is multiplied ×10, up to $1,000,000.
  • Winners keep their main-game cash plus any Super Rush prize. Returning champions add to their career total on the board.
